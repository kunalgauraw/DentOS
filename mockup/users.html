Role Permissions

Admin Owner / Manager

  • Full access to all features
  • User management & settings
  • All reports & analytics
  • Backup & restore
  • Discount: Unlimited

Dentist Primary Doctor

  • All patient & clinical features
  • Consultation, Rx, Treatment plans
  • Create invoices & collect payments
  • View own reports
  • Discount: Unlimited

Visiting Dentist Part-time Doctor

  • Clinical features (own patients only)
  • Consultation, Rx, Treatment plans
  • Create invoices (no discount)
  • No access to reports or settings
  • Discount: Not allowed

Receptionist Front Desk

  • Patient registration & search
  • Appointments booking
  • Billing & payment collection
  • Daily collection report (for cash reconciliation)
  • No access to monthly reports or doctor-wise data
  • Discount: Up to 10%
