# DentOS Product Vision

## The Operating System for Modern Dental Clinics

---

## Executive Summary

DentOS is a local-first, cloud-backed Dental Practice Management System (PMS) designed for small dental clinics.

**The goal is simple:**

> Enable a dental clinic to run completely digitally, from patient registration through consultation, prescription, treatment planning, billing, payments, receipts, and record retention, without depending on expensive SaaS subscriptions, cloud platforms, or third-party APIs.

The clinic should continue operating normally even if:

- Internet is down
- Cloud services are unavailable
- Messaging services are unavailable

while still providing:

- Automated backups
- Remote visibility in future versions
- Secure record retention
- Full data ownership

---

## Vision Statement

> To simplify dental practice management by bringing patient records, clinical workflows, and business operations into a single reliable system.

DentOS enables every dental clinic to operate digitally, efficiently, and independently through a simple, reliable platform that manages patient records, clinical care, treatment planning, and practice operations in one place.

---

## Product Positioning

**DentOS is not:**

- A hospital management system
- A CRM platform
- An ERP system
- A telemedicine platform

**DentOS is:**

> A local-first dental practice management system designed to help small dental clinics manage patient care and clinic operations from a single application.

---

## Product Principles

### 1. Local First

Patient care should never stop because the internet stopped.

- The clinic laptop remains the primary system
- Clinic operations must not depend on internet availability

### 2. One Source of Truth

Everything revolves around:

```
Patient → Visit → Clinical Record → Treatment → Billing
```

- No duplicate data entry
- No disconnected systems

### 3. Data Ownership

The clinic owns its data.

- Patient records, documents, and backups remain under the clinic's control
- Data can be exported at any time

### 4. Reliability

The system should continue operating during internet outages while protecting critical business and clinical information through backups, audit trails, and secure storage.

---

## Target Customer

### Primary Customer

Single dental clinic with typical setup:

```
1–3 Dentists
1 Receptionist
1 Billing Counter
```

Everything in the product is optimized for this operating model.

---

## Final Product Statement

> DentOS is a local-first dental practice management system that enables a dental clinic to manage patient records, consultations, prescriptions, treatment plans, billing, payments, receipts, and clinical documents through a single reliable application while maintaining full ownership of clinic data.
